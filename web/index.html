On the chain

    How it works

    A deal nobody can see, a death nobody can trace

    1

    Everyone shuffles

    Each seat re-encrypts the deck and reorders it through a network of ten switches. A proof shows every switch is honest: same cards, no extras, order unknown. One honest player is enough to keep the deal hidden from the rest.

    2

    Each seat reads one card

    Every other seat removes its layer from your card, and proves it did. The last layer is yours alone, so only you can read it.

    3

    Everyone acts at night

    Every living seat posts one sealed action of the same size. The wolf's names a victim. A villager's proves it names nobody. The contract can't tell them apart, and a villager can't forge a kill.

    4

    Only the total opens

    The contract adds the sealed actions together and the seats open the sum. The village learns who died. Nobody learns who did it.

Still public: that a table exists, the seats, the timing and the fees. Ghosts run in the tab of whoever started the game, so the privacy only means something between people. Read the threat model.
